# Govimithuru
Online trading flatform for farmers and buyers.

**Functional Requirements**
*Farmers should have ability to post their stocks.

*Farmers and buyers should have ability to create accounts their selves.

*Buyers should have ability to bid the price and request lower price.

*Both buyers and farmers can have Feedback.

*Both farmers and buyers should have ability to verify their accounts through NIC, but not mandotory.

*Verfied users must shows as verified users in product listings.

*All posts should be boostable by making a payment.

*Notifications

*Farmer should has ability to decide whether they dilivery the product or not.



**Non Functional Requirement**
*Initial website loading shouldn't take much time.
